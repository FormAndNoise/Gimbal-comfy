# Brand Identity & Standards — Gimbal

## 1. Product Summary & Identity
- **Product Name**: Gimbal (formerly Wayfinder / Latent Explorer)
- **Repository**: `gimbal-comfy`
- **Model**: Loose Endorsed Family (Form & Noise Atelier)
- **One-Sentence Invariant Job Line**:
  > *"Navigate latent space with precision flight instruments, not lottery prompts."*

---

## 2. Visual Mark & Geometry
- **Metaphor**: An artificial horizon / attitude indicator dial (circle enclosing horizontal datum bar, pitch index lines, and **one solid center heading pip**).
- **Canvas**: 24×24 unit square canvas (bounded to 20×20 optical area).
- **Stroke**: Exactly `1.75` units (`stroke-width="1.75"`), `fill="none"`, `stroke="currentColor"`.
- **Terminals & Joins**: `stroke-linecap="round"`, `stroke-linejoin="round"`.
- **The Family Fingerprint**: Exactly **one solid circular heading pip** (`r="1.3"`, `fill="currentColor"`, `stroke="none"`).

---

## 3. Color Tokens
- **Light Accent (Paper Ground)**: Instrument Teal `#0E8A8A`
- **Dark Mode Accent (Void Ground)**: `#35B8B8`
- **Ink Foreground (Light)**: `#141414`
- **Ink Foreground (Dark)**: `#F2EEE8`
- **Paper Ground (Light)**: `#F6F1EA`
- **Void Ground (Dark)**: `#0B0B0B`
- **Muted Hairline**: `#8A8680` (Dark: `#6B6763`)

---

## 4. Typography Stack
- **Wordmarks & Display**: Space Grotesk (Medium / SemiBold, tracking $-1\%$ to $-2\%$)
- **Interface & Body**: Inter (Regular / Medium)
